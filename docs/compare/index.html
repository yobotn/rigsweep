RigSweep compared

RigSweep is a free, open-source Windows app for developer PCs, so it overlaps only partly with general cleaners. These pages compare it with four well-known tools, fact by fact, and say where each one is the better choice. Competitor facts were checked on their official sites in November 2026.

  • RigSweep vs CCleaner: a general PC cleaner with paid plans, a registry cleaner and a driver updater.
  • RigSweep vs BleachBit: a free, open-source privacy cleaner for Windows and Linux.
  • RigSweep vs npkill: a free terminal tool that deletes node_modules and other build folders.
  • RigSweep vs PrivaZer: a free Windows privacy cleaner focused on usage traces.

What RigSweep does and doesn't do

  • It ends leftover dev servers, build daemons, MCP servers and agent CLIs, and deletes stale node_modules, build output and package caches. Nothing is ended or deleted without your click.
  • Its browser cleanup empties caches and keeps you signed in.
  • It has no registry cleaner, no RAM cleaner, no driver updater and no paid tier.
  • It measures RAM, CPU and processes before and after a cleanup and shows the real difference, with no made-up score.

For how-tos, see the guides.
